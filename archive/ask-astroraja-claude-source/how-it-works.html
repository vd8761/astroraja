Step by Step

The full process

01
Your Cosmic Identity

Enter Your Name, Raasi, Lagnam & Nakshatra

These four fields are the foundation of your entire reading. In Vedic astrology, your Raasi (Moon Sign) governs your emotional nature and inner world. Your Lagnam (Ascendant) reveals how you approach the world outwardly — your personality, physicality, and first impressions. Your Nakshatra (Birth Star) is the most precise indicator of all — a 27-fold division of the zodiac that reveals your core character, karmic tendencies, and life themes with remarkable accuracy.

You do not need your exact birth time to use this tool. If you already know your Raasi, Lagnam, and Nakshatra from a previous reading or family record, simply enter them. If you do not, our Raasi Guide page can help you find them.

03
AI Generation

Your Inputs Meet the Vedic Engine

All eight inputs — your name, Raasi, Lagnam, Nakshatra, and four answers — are sent together to our AI engine. The engine has been trained on a deeply crafted Vedic astrology system built by our founder over months of study. It applies authentic Vedic principles to your specific combination, not a generalised horoscope.

This process takes approximately 30 to 60 seconds. During this time, the AI is composing a structured, multi-section report that would take a human astrologer an hour or more to produce by hand.

04
Your Report

Read, Save & Download

Your report is displayed immediately in your browser — no account, no login required. Read it in full, then download it as a PDF to keep, print, or share with family. Or download as a Word document if you want to make personal notes alongside the guidance.
